## Ensemble for Orca

ADE's Team view (`src/flow/`) follows the data-flow model and diagram of
[Ensemble for Orca](https://github.com/DueGin/orca-ensemble): participants on the left, the parts of the
collaboration on the right, curved lines for the latest flows with a moving dot for the last 30 seconds, and
a feed of the latest flows. The line layout (measured anchors, spreading lines over a card's height, cubic
curves with arrowheads) is adapted from its `src/ui/run/diagram.tsx`.
